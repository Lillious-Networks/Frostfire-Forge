# Contributing

Project: Frostfire Forge

Contributions are welcome: bug reports, fixes, features and documentation.

## Before your first pull request: the CLA

This project has two licenses (see [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md)): it is free for noncommercial use, and commercial use needs a paid license from Lillious Networks. For that to be possible, every contributor signs the [Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md) once. You keep the copyright in your work; the agreement gives Lillious Networks the right to distribute it under both licenses, including the paid one, without payment to you.

Signing takes one comment:

1. Open your pull request.
2. A bot comments on it with a link to the agreement.
3. Reply with the exact sentence the bot shows.

The check on your pull request turns green and stays green for your later pull requests. A pull request cannot be merged until everyone who authored a commit in it has signed.

## Making a change

1. Fork the repository and make a branch for your change.
2. Keep the change focused: one fix or one feature per pull request.
3. Run the project's checks before you push. They are described in the README.
4. Describe what the change does and why in the pull request.

## Reporting a bug

Open an issue with what you did, what you expected and what happened, and the version you run.

## Security

Please do not report a security problem in a public issue. Contact Lillious Networks privately through https://github.com/Lillious-Networks first.
